Diffuse Background

absolute layer · linear-gradient + clip-path

The signature soft blue bloom. A full-bleed .absolute.inset-0.overflow-hidden container holds an absolutely-positioned gradient element that is clipped into a soft curved form with clip-path. Left: the literal effect (fades to white, as on a light hero). Right: the dark-theme variant used in this page's header (fades to transparent so the near-black canvas shows through).

Literal — fades to white
Dark variant — fades to transparent
Stops — rgba(219,234,254,0) 0% → rgba(200,225,255,.5) 25% → #60a5fa 65% → rgba(200,225,255,.5) 82% → #FFFFFF 100%, curved via clip-path: ellipse(85% 62% at 50% 118%).
